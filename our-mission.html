THE BARRENS FOUNDATION

Our mission.

Hospitality, recreation, and stewardship working together for Maine.

At The Barrens, we believe that true hospitality extends far beyond our cabins and trails - it begins with a deep, active commitment to the community and landscape we call home. Our vision is built on the idea that economic growth, outdoor recreation, and environmental stewardship must go hand in hand.

That commitment comes to life through The Barrens Foundation. Designed as a core pillar of our ecosystem, the Foundation is built on a simple, enduring promise: 1 percent of all sales from across the property go directly toward funding local grants throughout the state of Maine.

By partnering with local innovators, environmental stewards, community builders, and grassroots organizers, The Barrens Foundation focuses on:

  • Conserving Open Spaces: Protecting Maine's natural landscapes, wildlife corridors, and traditional working lands so future generations can continue to experience the outdoors.
  • Empowering Community and Culture: Supporting local arts programs, cultural initiatives, and regional projects that bring people together.
  • Expanding Outdoor Access: Funding infrastructure, trail maintenance, and recreation initiatives that keep nature inclusive, safe, and accessible to all.

When you stay, shop, or gather with us, you aren't just experiencing Maine - you are actively investing in its future. Every visit helps fuel the people, projects, and places that make our local communities thrive.
