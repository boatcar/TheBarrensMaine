U Pick field access opens August 2027 and is available by reservation only. Each two-hour picking window is limited to four vehicles, keeping the fields intimate and peaceful.

Standard exclusive field access / $15 per adultAges 13 and up. Children under 13 pick free. Standard access is shared with no more than three other vehicles during your time block. Each reservation is limited to one standard personal passenger vehicle. Cars, SUVs, and minivans are welcome. To protect the delicate barren ecosystem and manage local traffic, commercial passenger vans, Sprinter tour vans, and buses are not permitted on the property. Standard reservations allow up to five guests. Large families traveling together in one standard minivan or SUV may include up to eight guests.

Why we charge a field access feeField access is paid online when you reserve. This fee supports the specialized insurance and care required to safely open working land for experiential agritourism. It also lets us limit each block to just four vehicles, ensuring an unforgettable experience picking blueberries in the barrens.

FIELD TO ROAD

How your window works

  1. 01

    Check in

    Pull into your reserved space, show your booking confirmation, collect your field baskets, and head into the barrens.

  2. 02

    Pick // Up to 90 Minutes

    Gather as many berries as you want at your own pace. Your 2-hour reservation gives you 90 minutes of field picking time, leaving the remaining 30 minutes to run your harvest through the winnowing fans and check out at the stand.

  3. 03

    Winnow / 15 minutes

    Bring your harvest to the electric fan stations, where we will help remove leaves, stems, and twigs.

  4. 04

    Weigh and roll / 15 minutes

    We weigh the winnowed berries at $5 per pound, pack your take home containers, and check out any farm stand goods.
