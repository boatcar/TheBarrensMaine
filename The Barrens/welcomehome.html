The land
comes first.

The Barrens is a working wild blueberry farm on 21 acres in Stockton Springs, Maine. We are preparing the fields, converting our two bay garage into a public farm stand, and building a reservation only U Pick experience that keeps the land quiet and uncrowded.

Ways to visit

A small place by design.

01

U Pick blueberries

Field entry is $15 for adults, children under 13 pick free, and winnowed berries are $5 per pound. Each two-hour window is limited to four personal passenger vehicles, with up to five guests per reservation. Large families traveling together in one standard minivan or SUV may include up to eight guests.

U PICK DETAILS →

02

The farm stand

Open to the public from our existing two bay garage. During blueberry harvest season, from late July through August, visitors will use street parking on a first come, first served basis. Check the website before visiting for current parking information.

HOW IT WILL WORK →

03

The skate lounge

A reservable mini ramp for individuals and small groups. Every skater must complete the appropriate waiver.
