01

Visiting The Barrens

We are in Stockton Springs, Maine, on Blueberry Lane just off Route 1A. The property is about two miles from Scenic Route 1 and five miles inland from Penobscot Bay. Full arrival directions will be included with U Pick reservations and posted with public farm stand hours.

We are not open to the public yet. Beginning in August 2027, the farm stand is planned to open Monday through Saturday and remain closed Sunday. U Pick will operate through reserved two-hour windows Monday through Saturday. Exact daily hours will be posted as opening approaches and may change with the crop and coastal weather.

Our first public U Pick season and planned farm stand opening are set for August 2027. The crop and coastal weather will determine the exact opening date, daily picking conditions, and final hours. We will post those details here before reservations open.

The Barrens is our home and working homestead on 21 acres in Stockton Springs, Maine. Eight acres are native wild lowbush blueberry barrens, with thirteen acres of coastal woods behind them. We are preparing the property for our first public U Pick season, roadside farm stand, and skate lounge in August 2027.

02

U Pick operations

Yes. U Pick field access is available by advance reservation only. Reservations keep each two-hour window to no more than four vehicles, so the barrens remain quiet and there is a dedicated parking space for every picking party. Online booking is still in progress.

Standard field access is $15 for each guest age 13 and older. Children under 13 enter free. After picking, cleaned and winnowed berries are weighed and purchased separately for $5 per pound. The private Barrens Pass is $300 for one party to have the field and skate lounge to themselves during a two-hour window.

Each standard reservation covers one personal passenger vehicle and up to five guests, with at least one adult in the party. Large families traveling together in one standard minivan or SUV may include up to eight guests. Commercial passenger vans, Sprinter tour vans, and buses are not permitted. Standard reservations share the field with no more than three other vehicles. A Private Barrens Pass reserves the full field window for one vehicle and up to five guests.

Plan on up to 90 minutes in the field. The remaining 30 minutes are for bringing your harvest to the winnowing fans, weighing the cleaned berries, packing them for home, and checking out at the farm stand. We provide field baskets when you check in.

Every U Pick reservation includes one of four parking spaces on the property. Those spaces are held for active field reservations during harvest season. Additional vehicles cannot be added to a reservation, so each party should arrive together in one vehicle.

Cancel more than 72 hours before your reservation for a full refund. Reservations canceled within 72 hours are nonrefundable. If we close the fields because coastal weather makes picking unsafe, you may choose a full refund or priority rescheduling at no additional cost.

03

Farm stand and skate lounge

Yes. No reservation is required for the farm stand during posted public hours. During blueberry harvest season, farm stand visitors must use available street parking because the four spaces on the property are reserved for active picking parties. The stand will offer fresh and frozen wild blueberries as the crop allows, seasonal goods, small-batch merchandise, and work from independent makers.

No. It is a small indoor miniramp inside the farm operation. Visitors may hang out in the lounge during posted farm stand hours, subject to capacity and farm operations, and private skate sessions will be available by reservation. Every skater must sign the digital liability waiver before entering the ramp area. Helmets and pads are strongly recommended.

04

Land, community, and what comes next

We are using strict organic agricultural practices, but the barrens are not currently certified organic. Our goal is to secure MOFGA Organic Certification by 2029. Until that process is complete, we will describe how we grow and manage the crop plainly rather than use a certification we have not yet earned.

After operating costs are covered, we direct 10 percent of net profits from the U Pick blueberry experience and 1 percent of all other net profits from the Farm Stand toward local grants, community projects, and kids' skate initiatives in Maine. The farm must remain healthy for that support to be lasting, so giving grows with the success of the land and business.

First, it looks like a healthy working farm: a thoughtful U Pick operation, a useful roadside stand, and a small skate lounge run responsibly. Beyond that, our long-term vision explores outdoor recreation, gatherings around art and music, and a small hospitality retreat. Those ideas are possibilities, not amenities or opening dates we can promise today. We will build in phases and let the land, permits, funding, and real use of the property guide what comes next.
