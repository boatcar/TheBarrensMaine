Questions, answered.

A few useful details about the place we are building.

The farm stand is planned as part of the first phase of the project, with opening details announced as construction and operations come into focus.

The action sports park is a private, on-site compound reserved exclusively for resort guests, featuring:

World-Class Infrastructure: A custom concrete flow skatepark, the first Evergreen Skatepark in New England, and a dedicated 13 acre mountain bike trail network and bike park with machine cut singletrack and progression features.

Four-Season Activation: The action sports infrastructure transitions across seasons, incorporating a seasonal winter outdoor ice rink and a future progression tow rope terrain park for skiing and snowboarding.

Community Access: While private for guests, the park opens to the public once a month for "The Blueberry Jam," a curated music, arts, and action sports gathering.

The Barrens is a future roadside farm stand, cultural gathering space, action sports destination, and micro resort rooted in Maine's native lowbush wild barrens.

The land is located 2 miles from the Scenic Route 1 and 1A junction in Stockton Springs, Maine, connected to the landscape, communities, and outdoor culture of the region.

Blueberry Jam is a planned monthly public weekend celebrating music, art, skateboarding, mountain biking, and the outdoors.

The Barrens Foundation will direct 1 percent of sales from across the property toward local grants throughout Maine.
