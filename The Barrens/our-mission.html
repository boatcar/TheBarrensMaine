THE BARRENS FOUNDATION

Our mission.

Lowbush barrens, local agriculture, and community working together for Maine.

Out here on these 21 wild acres, we aren't just growing berries. We're building a farm that gives back to the community around it while caring for the land that makes it possible.

Phase one starts now with our roadside farm stand and first public U Pick wild blueberry season. But that's only the beginning. The larger vision includes a working farm stand, a curated retail and event space, and eventually, a micro resort where people can stay and experience the barrens and local ecosystem for themselves.

We believe giving back begins with building a farm that can thrive for the long term. After ordinary operating costs are covered, we direct 10 percent of net profits from blueberry sales and 1 percent of net profits from all other farm stand sales to community work through The Barrens Foundation. This keeps the business healthy, allows us to keep investing in the land, and lets the community share in our success.

We're starting with something tangible: helping local kids get their first skateboard or bike. Over time, as the business grows, so will our ability to fund small grants for Mainers working in music and art, conservation, trail stewardship, and other efforts that keep Maine wild and its outdoor culture thriving.

When you pull off the road for a pint of berries, spend an afternoon picking your own, or find something else at the stand, your purchase helps care for this land and build a resilient agricultural business. As the farm succeeds, a share of its profits returns to the community we call home.
