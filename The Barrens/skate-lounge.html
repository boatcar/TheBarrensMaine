NOT A PUBLIC SKATEPARK

Keep it small. Keep it responsible.

The skate lounge is a compact indoor miniramp inside the farm operation. It is a place for individuals and small groups to skate, spend time together, and connect the board culture behind The Barrens with the land outside.

No reservation is required to hang out in the lounge during posted farm stand hours. The miniramp will also be available for private skate rentals by reservation. Access may be limited by capacity, rentals, maintenance, or farm operations, so check the website before making a special trip.

EVERY SKATER SIGNS FIRSTAll participants must complete the digital liability waiver at the shop counter before entering the ramp area. No exceptions.

BEFORE YOU DROP IN

Know your limits.

Skateboarding is inherently dangerous. The miniramp is a skate at your own risk feature. Skate within your ability, take turns, and respect the people sharing the room.

Helmets and pads are highly recommended. Minors must follow the waiver and supervision requirements posted at the farm stand.

ACCESS AND AVAILABILITY

The farm still comes first.

Posted hours Lounge access follows current farm stand hours unless a closure is posted.

Private rentals Individuals and small groups will be able to reserve the miniramp for a private session.

Updates Check the website for closures, rental sessions, and current access before visiting.
