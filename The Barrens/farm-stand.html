BUILT FROM WHAT IS HERE

A small roadside stop.

No reservation is required to visit the farm stand. The existing two bay garage will become a simple public space for fresh and frozen wild blueberries, seasonal goods from the property, small batch merchandise, and work from independent makers.

Come by after a morning harvest, pack your berries, browse the stand, or spend a little more time at The Barrens. Product availability will follow the crop, the season, and what we can produce responsibly.

PLANNED OPENING / AUGUST 2027We plan to remain open Monday through Saturday before, during, and after blueberry harvest season. Closed Sunday.

HARVEST SEASON PARKING

Check before you pull in.

Street parking is required only during blueberry harvest season, from late July through August, and is available on a first come, first served basis. The four spaces on the property are held for active field reservations during those weeks.

Outside harvest season, parking instructions may change with field conditions and work on the property. Check the website before visiting for the latest information.

WHAT TO EXPECT
