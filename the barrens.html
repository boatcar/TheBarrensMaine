Stockton Springs Agro-Ecosystem

The Wild Lowbush Barrens

21 acres of historic coastal Maine terrain sitting on Route 1A. Dual-purpose topography uniting high-speed gravity trail corridors with heritage wild blueberry production.

Topography & Trail Integration

The property transitions from sweeping, sun-drenched upland ridges down into deep evergreen timber buffers.

  • Natural granite outcrops and sweeping organic soil lines.
  • Open sightlines across the active barrens minimizing blind trail transitions.
  • Configured specifically to support all-season utility equipment and Kubota L02 series maintenance access.

Agri-Operations & Harvest

Balancing active trail infrastructure with high-yield wild lowbush blueberry (Vaccinium angustifolium) cultivation.

  • Hand-rake and commercial management protocols across open parcels.
  • Flash-freezing and sustainable wholesale paper-pulp packaging tracks.
  • Integrated land management preserving native Maine flora alongside recreational zones.
